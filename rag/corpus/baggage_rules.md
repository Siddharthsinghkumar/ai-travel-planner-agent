# Baggage Rules — Knowledge Base

## Carry-On Dimensions and Weight by Carrier

1. American Airlines allows one carry-on bag with maximum dimensions of 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 36 x 23 cm) and one personal item. The carry-on must fit in the overhead bin. Weight is not restricted for domestic flights but international flights may have a 10 kg (22 lb) limit depending on the destination.

2. Delta Air Lines permits one carry-on bag up to 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 36 x 23 cm) plus one personal item. Delta does not enforce a specific weight limit for carry-on bags on domestic flights, but the bag must be light enough for the passenger to lift into the overhead bin unassisted.

3. United Airlines allows one carry-on bag with maximum dimensions of 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 36 x 23 cm) and one personal item. Basic Economy passengers on United are restricted to one personal item only that fits under the seat (9 x 10 x 17 inches) unless they are a Premier member, primary cardholder of a qualifying MileagePlus card, or traveling on a transatlantic flight.

4. Lufthansa permits one carry-on bag with maximum dimensions of 55 x 40 x 23 cm and a maximum weight of 8 kg in Economy Class. Business and First Class passengers may carry two bags of the same dimensions and weight. Additionally, one personal item (laptop bag, handbag) is allowed.

5. Emirates allows one carry-on bag with maximum dimensions of 55 x 38 x 20 cm. Economy Class passengers are limited to 7 kg (15 lb), while Business and First Class passengers may carry up to two pieces with a combined weight not exceeding 12 kg (26 lb) for Business and 15 kg (33 lb) for First Class.

6. Singapore Airlines permits one carry-on bag with maximum dimensions of 115 cm total (55 x 40 x 23 cm). Economy Standard passengers are limited to 7 kg, Economy Flexi and above may carry up to two pieces with a combined weight of 15 kg. Business and First Class passengers may carry two pieces with a combined weight of 15 kg.

7. British Airways allows one carry-on bag with maximum dimensions of 56 x 45 x 25 cm and a maximum weight of 23 kg in all cabins, plus one laptop bag or handbag. This is notably more generous than most carriers in terms of weight allowance for carry-on.

## Checked Baggage Allowances by Cabin Class

8. American Airlines Economy passengers on domestic flights receive one free checked bag on transatlantic and transpacific routes. Domestic Economy Basic fares do not include a free checked bag. Business Class includes two free checked bags up to 32 kg (70 lb) each on international routes.

9. Delta Air Lines Basic Economy fares include no free checked bags on domestic routes. Main Cabin and above receive one free checked bag on domestic flights (up to 23 kg / 50 lb). Delta One and First Class include two free checked bags up to 32 kg (70 lb) each.

10. United Airlines Economy passengers on domestic flights pay for the first checked bag ($35 for the first bag, $45 for the second). Premier Silver and above receive at least one free checked bag. Polaris Business Class includes two free checked bags up to 32 kg (70 lb) each.

11. Lufthansa Economy Light fares do not include any checked baggage. Economy Classic and Flex fares include one checked bag up to 23 kg. Business Class includes two checked bags up to 32 kg each. First Class includes three checked bags up to 32 kg each.

12. Emirates Economy passengers receive 20-30 kg of checked baggage depending on the fare type (Saver, Flex, Flex Plus). Business Class passengers receive 40 kg, and First Class passengers receive 50 kg. Baggage is calculated by total weight rather than piece concept on most routes.

13. Singapore Airlines Economy Lite fares may not include checked baggage on some routes. Standard Economy includes 25 kg, while Business Class includes 40 kg and First/Suites Class includes 50 kg. KrisFlyer elite members receive additional baggage allowances of 10-20 kg depending on tier.

14. British Airways Economy (Hand Baggage Only) fares include no checked baggage. Standard Economy includes one checked bag up to 23 kg. Business Class (Club Europe/World) includes two checked bags up to 32 kg each. First Class includes two checked bags up to 32 kg each.

## Oversize and Overweight Fees

15. American Airlines charges $100-$200 for overweight bags (23-32 kg / 51-70 lb) and $200 for bags over 32 kg. Oversize bags (62-126 linear inches) incur a $150-$200 fee. Bags exceeding 126 linear inches or 45 kg are not accepted as checked baggage.

16. Delta Air Lines charges $100 for overweight bags (23-32 kg / 51-70 lb) within the US and $200 for international overweight bags. Oversize bags (62-115 linear inches) incur a $200 fee. Delta does not accept bags over 32 kg or 115 linear inches.

17. United Airlines charges $100 for the first overweight bag (23-32 kg) and $200 for each additional overweight bag. Oversize fees of $200 apply for bags 62-115 linear inches. Bags over 32 kg or 115 linear inches must be shipped as cargo.

18. Lufthansa charges EUR 70-100 for overweight bags (23-32 kg) depending on the route. Oversize bags (over 158 cm total dimensions) incur a EUR 100-150 fee. Sports equipment is charged separately at EUR 50-150 per item depending on the type and route.

19. Emirates charges USD 75-200 per extra kilogram over the free allowance, depending on the route. Oversize items (over 300 cm total dimensions) require prior approval and may incur additional handling fees of USD 150-300.

## Restricted and Prohibited Items

20. Lithium-ion batteries with a watt-hour rating exceeding 100 Wh are prohibited in checked baggage and may be carried in carry-on baggage only with airline approval. Spare lithium batteries must be individually protected to prevent short circuits by placing in original retail packaging or taping over terminals.

21. Lithium metal batteries are limited to 2 grams of lithium content per battery in carry-on baggage. They are prohibited in checked baggage. Power banks are considered spare lithium batteries and must be carried in the cabin only.

22. Flammable liquids, explosives, compressed gases, and oxidizing materials are prohibited in both carry-on and checked baggage. This includes lighter fluid, fireworks, spray paint, and bleach. Small arms ammunition may be transported in checked baggage only with proper declaration and packaging.

23. Sharp objects including knives, box cutters, ice picks, and swords are prohibited in carry-on baggage but may be packed in checked baggage. Sporting goods such as baseball bats, golf clubs, and hockey sticks must be checked. Tools longer than 7 inches (measured from end to end) must be checked.

24. Liquids, aerosols, and gels in carry-on baggage are limited to containers of 100 ml (3.4 oz) or less, all fitting within one transparent resealable bag of approximately 1 liter capacity. This is the 3-1-1 rule enforced by TSA and most international security agencies. Medications and baby food are exempt but must be declared.

25. Firearms are prohibited in carry-on baggage under all circumstances. Firearms may be transported in checked baggage only if unloaded, declared to the airline at check-in, and locked in a hard-sided container. Ammunition must be securely packed in the original manufacturer's packaging or equivalent.

26. E-cigarettes and vaping devices are prohibited in checked baggage and must be carried in the cabin. They must not be used or charged on board the aircraft. Some countries and airports prohibit the possession of vaping devices entirely — travelers should verify destination regulations.

27. Perishable items such as fresh fruits, vegetables, meat, and dairy products may be restricted or prohibited by the destination country's customs regulations regardless of airline policy. Travelers are responsible for compliance with agricultural import restrictions.

28. Cash exceeding USD 10,000 (or equivalent) must be declared to customs upon entry or exit from most countries. Failure to declare may result in confiscation and penalties. There is no limit on the amount of cash that can be carried, only a declaration requirement.
