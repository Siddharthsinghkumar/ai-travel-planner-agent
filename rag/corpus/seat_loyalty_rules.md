# Seat Selection and Loyalty Rules — Knowledge Base

## Seat Selection Fees by Fare Class

1. American Airlines: Main Cabin passengers can select standard seats for free at booking. Preferred seats (extra legroom, front of cabin) cost USD 20-150 depending on the route and seat location. Main Cabin Extra seats with additional legroom cost USD 35-200. Basic Economy passengers are assigned seats at check-in and cannot select seats in advance unless they pay a fee.

2. Delta Air Lines: Main Cabin passengers can select standard seats for free at booking. Comfort+ seats (extra legroom, priority boarding) cost USD 30-200 depending on the route. Preferred seats (front of cabin) cost USD 15-100. Basic Economy passengers are assigned seats at check-in and cannot change their seat assignment before check-in.

3. United Airlines: Economy passengers can select standard seats for free at booking. Economy Plus seats (extra legroom) cost USD 20-250 depending on the route and seat location. Preferred seats cost USD 15-120. Basic Economy passengers are assigned seats at check-in and cannot select seats in advance. United offers a same-day confirmed change for a fee of USD 75 for non-elite members.

4. Lufthansa: Economy Classic and Flex passengers can select standard seats for free at booking. Preferred seats cost EUR 20-80. Extra legroom seats cost EUR 50-150. Economy Light passengers must pay for all seat selection, with standard seats costing EUR 15-50. Business Class passengers can select any seat for free.

5. Emirates: Economy passengers can select standard seats for free at booking. Preferred seats (front of cabin, bulkhead) cost USD 20-100. Extra legroom seats cost USD 50-200. Business and First Class passengers can select any seat for free, including the onboard lounge and shower spa areas on A380 aircraft.

6. Singapore Airlines: Economy passengers can select standard seats for free at booking. Extra legroom seats cost USD 30-150 depending on the route. Preferred seats (forward cabin) cost USD 20-80. Business and First/Suites Class passengers can select any seat for free. KrisFlyer elite members receive complimentary seat selection even in Economy Lite fares.

7. British Airways: Economy passengers can select standard seats for free from 24 hours before departure (online check-in). Advance seat selection costs GBP 8-60 depending on the seat location and route. Extra legroom seats cost GBP 25-100. Business Class (Club Europe/World) passengers can select any seat for free.

## Status-Based Perks by Tier

8. American Airlines AAdvantage: Gold members receive complimentary preferred seat selection, priority check-in, and one free checked bag. Platinum members receive complimentary Main Cabin Extra seat selection, priority boarding, and two free checked bags. Platinum Pro members receive complimentary upgrades on domestic flights (space-available) and three free checked bags. Executive Platinum members receive complimentary upgrades on domestic and short-haul international flights, free same-day standby, and three free checked bags.

9. Delta SkyMiles: Silver Medallion members receive complimentary Comfort+ seat selection at check-in, priority check-in, and one free checked bag. Gold Medallion members receive complimentary Comfort+ seat selection at booking, Sky Priority boarding, and two free checked bags. Platinum Medallion members receive complimentary Delta One upgrades on domestic flights (space-available), free same-day confirmed changes, and three free checked bags. Diamond Medallion members receive complimentary upgrades on all domestic and short-haul international routes, Choice Benefits, and four free checked bags.

10. United MileagePlus: Premier Silver members receive complimentary Economy Plus seat selection at check-in, priority check-in, and one free checked bag. Premier Gold members receive complimentary Economy Plus seat selection at booking, Premier Access boarding, and two free checked bags. Premier Platinum members receive complimentary United Plus upgrades on domestic flights (space-available), free same-day confirmed changes, and three free checked bags. Premier 1K members receive complimentary Global Upgrade certificates, free same-day standby, and three free checked bags.

11. Lufthansa Miles & More: Frequent Traveller members receive complimentary preferred seat selection and priority check-in. Senator members receive complimentary extra legroom seat selection, lounge access, and two free checked bags (additional to standard allowance). HON Circle members receive complimentary First Class lounge access, First Class terminal access, and three free checked bags.

12. Emirates Skywards: Silver members receive complimentary preferred seat selection, priority check-in, and additional baggage allowance of 10 kg. Gold members receive complimentary extra legroom seat selection, lounge access, and additional baggage allowance of 15 kg. Platinum members receive complimentary First Class lounge access, additional baggage allowance of 20 kg, and complimentary chauffeur drive service.

13. Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer: Silver members receive complimentary preferred seat selection and additional baggage allowance of 10 kg. Gold members receive complimentary extra legroom seat selection, lounge access, and additional baggage allowance of 20 kg. PPS Solitaire members receive complimentary First Class lounge access, priority waitlist clearance, and additional baggage allowance of 30 kg.

## Lounge Access Tiers

14. American Airlines Admirals Club: Access is available to Admirals Club members (annual membership USD 650), AAdvantage ConciergeKey members, and passengers traveling in First or Business Class on transcontinental or international flights. OneWorld Emerald members have access when traveling on any OneWorld flight. Day passes are available for USD 59.

15. Delta Sky Club: Access is available to Sky Club members (annual membership USD 650), Delta One passengers, and passengers traveling in Business Class on international or transcontinental flights. American Express Platinum cardholders receive access when flying Delta. Day passes are available for USD 59.

16. United Club: Access is available to United Club members (annual membership USD 650), United Polaris passengers, and Star Alliance Gold members traveling on a Star Alliance flight. Day passes are available for USD 59. United Club Infinity Card holders receive access when flying United.

## Miles Redemption Rules

17. American Airlines AAdvantage miles can be redeemed for award flights starting at 7,500 miles for short-haul domestic one-way flights. International award flights start at 20,000 miles one-way for Europe and 30,000 miles one-way for Asia. Miles do not expire as long as the account has activity (earning or redemption) at least once every 24 months.

18. Delta SkyMiles redemption rates are dynamic and vary based on demand, route, and cabin class. Domestic award flights typically start at 5,000 miles one-way but can exceed 50,000 miles for peak dates. International awards start at 25,000 miles one-way for Europe and 35,000 miles for Asia. SkyMiles do not expire.

19. United MileagePlus award flights start at 5,000 miles for short-haul domestic one-way flights. International awards start at 15,000 miles one-way for Europe and 25,000 miles for Asia. United offers Excursionist Perk, which provides a free one-way award within a multi-city itinerary when traveling between different regions. Miles do not expire.

20. Partner award booking: Most airline loyalty programs allow miles to be redeemed for flights on partner airlines within the same alliance (OneWorld, Star Alliance, SkyTeam). Partner award availability is often more limited than the airline's own flights. Booking partner awards typically requires calling the airline's reservation line and may incur additional booking fees.

## Family Seating Policies

21. American Airlines: Children under 15 traveling with an adult on the same reservation will be seated together whenever possible. If seats are not available together, American Airlines will attempt to reassign seats at no charge. Families can call reservations to request adjacent seating. Basic Economy passengers may be separated and must pay for seat selection to guarantee adjacent seats.

22. Delta Air Lines: Delta will not intentionally separate children under 15 from an adult on the same reservation. If the system cannot assign adjacent seats, Delta agents will attempt to rearrange seating at the gate or on board. Families can call Delta to request adjacent seating. Basic Economy passengers are assigned seats at check-in and may be separated.

23. United Airlines: United will not intentionally separate children under 12 from an adult on the same reservation. United's seating system attempts to assign adjacent seats automatically. If adjacent seats are not available, United agents will attempt to rearrange seating. Families can call United to request adjacent seating. Basic Economy passengers may be separated.

24. Lufthansa: Children under 12 traveling with an adult are guaranteed adjacent seating at no additional cost. If adjacent seats are not available at booking, Lufthansa will reassign seats before departure. Families can contact Lufthansa to ensure adjacent seating. Economy Light passengers may need to pay for seat selection if adjacent seats are not available in the free allocation.

25. British Airways: Children under 12 will be seated next to at least one adult in their party at no additional cost. British Airways' booking system automatically attempts to assign adjacent seats. If adjacent seats are not available, families can contact British Airways to request reassignment. Seat selection fees may apply for specific seat preferences beyond the free allocation.

26. Emirates: Children under 12 traveling with an adult are seated together whenever possible. Emirates' booking system attempts to assign adjacent seats automatically. If adjacent seats are not available, families can contact Emirates to request reassignment. Seat selection fees may apply for preferred or extra legroom seats. Emirates offers family seating zones on some aircraft.

27. Singapore Airlines: Children under 12 traveling with an adult are seated together whenever possible. Singapore Airlines' booking system attempts to assign adjacent seats automatically. KrisFlyer members receive priority for family seating. If adjacent seats are not available, families can contact Singapore Airlines to request reassignment.
