# Travel Policy Rules — Knowledge Base

## Booking Windows

1. Domestic flights should be booked at least 14 days in advance for best pricing. Last-minute bookings within 48 hours may incur a 15-30% surcharge. Group bookings of 10 or more passengers require a minimum of 45 days advance notice to ensure adequate seat availability.

2. International flights should be booked at least 30 days in advance for best pricing. Holiday season bookings (December 15 through January 10) require 60 days advance notice due to peak demand. Airlines typically release seats 11 months ahead, so booking early maximizes choice.

3. Corporate travel bookings must go through the approved travel management system. Employees are required to select economy class unless the flight duration exceeds 8 hours, in which case premium economy is permitted. All corporate bookings must include a cost-center code for billing purposes.

## Cancellation Policies

4. Free cancellation is available up to 24 hours after booking for all flights. After the 24-hour window, cancellations within 7 days of departure incur a 25% fee. Cancellations within 48 hours of departure incur a 50% fee on the total ticket value.

5. Non-refundable tickets cannot be cancelled but may be converted to travel credit. Travel credit is valid for 12 months from the original booking date and can only be used by the original passenger. Credit must be applied to a new booking of equal or greater value; any difference is charged at the time of rebooking.

6. Partial cancellations removing one passenger from a group booking are not permitted. The entire booking must be cancelled and rebooked if any changes are needed. This policy prevents fare discrepancies that arise when group rates are split.

## Baggage Allowances

7. Economy class includes 1 checked bag weighing up to 23 kg and 1 carry-on bag weighing up to 7 kg. Business class includes 2 checked bags of 32 kg each and 2 carry-ons of 10 kg each. First class includes 3 checked bags of 32 kg each and 2 carry-ons of 15 kg each.

8. Hand-baggage-only fares exclude all checked baggage. Excess baggage costs ₹500 per kilogram above the free allowance. Sports equipment such as golf clubs or skis requires advance booking and incurs a flat ₹2,000 fee per item.

9. Infants under 2 years are entitled to 10 kg of checked baggage free of charge in addition to a collapsible stroller. Children aged 2-12 receive the same baggage allowance as adults for their fare class. Unaccompanied minors receive an additional 5 kg allowance for comfort items.

## Price Change and Refund Rules

10. Price drop protection applies if the fare drops within 24 hours of booking. The difference is refunded as travel credit to the original passenger account. This protection does not apply to promotional fares or flash sales.

11. Refunds are processed within 7-10 business days to the original payment method. International refunds may take up to 21 business days depending on the issuing bank. Refund processing times are estimates and not guaranteed.

12. Name changes on tickets incur a ₹1,500 fee per passenger and must be requested at least 72 hours before departure. Name changes are not permitted within 72 hours of departure for security reasons. The new passenger name must match the travel document exactly.

## Seat Selection and Upgrades

13. Seat selection is complimentary for business and first class passengers. Economy class passengers may select standard seats for ₹300 or preferred seats (extra legroom, front cabin) for ₹800. Seat selection must be completed at least 24 hours before departure or seats are auto-assigned.

14. Upgrades from economy to business class are subject to availability and require payment of the fare difference plus a ₹3,000 processing fee. Upgrade requests can be made at booking, during online check-in, or at the airport gate. Airport upgrades are offered at a discounted rate if seats remain unsold 2 hours before departure.

15. Exit row seats require passengers to be at least 15 years old and physically capable of assisting in an emergency. Passengers with reduced mobility, pregnant travelers, or those traveling with infants cannot be seated in exit rows. These seats carry a ₹500 premium over standard economy seats.

## Visa and Documentation Requirements

16. Passengers must carry a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond the return date for all international travel. Some countries require a minimum of 3 months validity; travelers should verify destination-specific requirements. Passport photocopies should be kept separately from the original document.

17. Visa requirements vary by destination and nationality. Travelers are solely responsible for obtaining valid visas before departure. The booking platform provides visa requirement information as a convenience but does not guarantee accuracy. Visa processing typically takes 5-15 business days.

18. Travel insurance is mandatory for all international bookings and costs ₹800 per person per trip. The policy covers medical emergencies up to ₹50 lakh, trip cancellation, and baggage loss. Domestic travel insurance is optional but recommended at ₹200 per person per trip.

19. Yellow fever vaccination certificate is required for travel to Sub-Saharan Africa and parts of South America. The certificate must be issued at least 10 days before travel to be valid. Some countries also require proof of polio or hepatitis A vaccination.

20. Schengen area travelers must provide proof of accommodation and return flight at border control. Travelers should carry printed copies of hotel reservations and flight itineraries. Failure to provide documentation may result in denial of entry.

## Loyalty Program Rules

21. Silver tier members earn 1.5x points on all bookings and receive priority check-in. Gold tier members earn 2x points, priority boarding, and one free checked bag upgrade per trip. Platinum tier members earn 3x points, free lounge access, and complimentary seat selection on all flights.

22. Points expire after 24 months of account inactivity. A single qualifying flight resets the expiration clock for all accumulated points. Points can be redeemed for flight upgrades at a rate of 10,000 points equaling ₹5,000 in fare value.

23. Points cannot be transferred between accounts or converted to cash. Family pooling allows up to 4 family members to combine points into a single account. The primary account holder controls all redemption decisions for pooled points.

## Weather and Seasonal Policies

24. Monsoon season (June through September) flights to coastal destinations may experience delays of 1-3 hours. Airlines may reroute flights through alternate airports during severe weather. Passengers are notified via SMS and email of any schedule changes at least 2 hours before departure.

25. Winter season (December through February) flights to northern destinations may require de-icing, adding 15-30 minutes to departure times. Snowfall advisories in mountainous regions may require switching to alternate airports. Airlines provide complimentary hot beverages during extended ground delays.

26. Summer season (April through June) is peak travel time with 20-40% higher fares on popular routes. Early morning and late evening flights typically have lower demand and better pricing. Booking mid-week flights (Tuesday through Thursday) can save 10-15% compared to weekend travel.

27. Cyclone alerts may result in automatic rebooking at no additional cost. The airline reserves the right to cancel flights with less than 24 hours notice for safety reasons. Affected passengers receive full refunds or free rebooking on the next available flight.

## Special Assistance

28. Wheelchair assistance must be requested at least 48 hours before departure. The airline provides wheelchair service from check-in to the aircraft door and from the aircraft to baggage claim. Passengers requiring oxygen must arrange it through the airline at least 72 hours before departure.

29. Unaccompanied minor service is available for children aged 5-12 at ₹2,500 per flight segment. The service includes dedicated staff escort, priority boarding, and in-flight supervision. Children under 5 cannot travel unaccompanied under any circumstances.

30. Service animals travel free of charge in the cabin with proper documentation. Documentation must include a current veterinary health certificate and proof of service animal training. Emotional support animals are not classified as service animals and must travel as checked baggage.

31. Pregnant travelers beyond 36 weeks require a medical clearance certificate to fly. The certificate must be issued within 72 hours of travel and state the expected due date. Twin or multiple pregnancies require clearance after 32 weeks.

## Refund and Compensation Policies

32. Flight delays exceeding 3 hours entitle passengers to compensation per DGCA regulations. Domestic delays of 3-6 hours receive ₹5,000 or a free return ticket. Delays exceeding 6 hours receive ₹10,000 or a free return ticket plus hotel accommodation if an overnight stay is required.

33. Denied boarding due to overbooking entitles passengers to ₹20,000 or 200% of the one-way fare, whichever is higher. The airline must also provide a free rebooking on the next available flight or a full refund. Passengers may choose between monetary compensation and travel credit of equal value.

34. Lost baggage claims must be filed within 7 days of arrival at the destination. The airline liability for domestic lost baggage is capped at ₹50,000 per passenger. International lost baggage claims are governed by the Montreal Convention with a limit of 1,288 SDR (approximately ₹1.4 lakh).

35. Trip cancellation due to medical emergency receives a full refund with a valid medical certificate. Natural disasters and government travel advisories also qualify for full refunds. Personal reasons such as change of plans or work conflicts do not qualify for refunds beyond the standard cancellation policy.
