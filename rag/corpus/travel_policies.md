# Travel Policy Rules — Knowledge Base

## Booking Windows
1. Domestic flights should be booked at least 14 days in advance for best pricing.
2. International flights should be booked at least 30 days in advance for best pricing.
3. Last-minute bookings (within 48 hours) may incur a 15-30% surcharge.
4. Group bookings (10+ passengers) require a minimum of 45 days advance notice.
5. Holiday season bookings (Dec 15 – Jan 10) require 60 days advance notice.

## Cancellation Policies
6. Free cancellation is available up to 24 hours after booking for all flights.
7. Cancellations within 7 days of departure incur a 25% fee.
8. Cancellations within 48 hours of departure incur a 50% fee.
9. Non-refundable tickets cannot be cancelled but may be converted to travel credit.
10. Travel credit is valid for 12 months from the original booking date.

## Baggage Allowances
11. Economy class includes 1 checked bag (23 kg) and 1 carry-on (7 kg).
12. Business class includes 2 checked bags (32 kg each) and 2 carry-ons (10 kg each).
13. Hand-baggage-only fares exclude all checked baggage; excess baggage costs ₹500/kg.
14. Sports equipment (golf clubs, skis) requires advance booking and incurs a ₹2,000 fee.
15. Infants (under 2 years) are entitled to 10 kg of checked baggage free of charge.

## Price Change and Refund Rules
16. Price drop protection: if the fare drops within 24 hours of booking, the difference is refunded as travel credit.
17. Refunds are processed within 7-10 business days to the original payment method.
18. Partial cancellations (removing one passenger from a group) are not permitted; the entire booking must be rebooked.
19. Name changes on tickets incur a ₹1,500 fee per passenger and must be requested at least 72 hours before departure.
20. Upgrades from economy to business class are subject to availability and require payment of the fare difference plus a ₹3,000 processing fee.

## Loyalty Program Rules
21. Silver tier members earn 1.5x points on all bookings.
22. Gold tier members earn 2x points and receive priority boarding.
23. Platinum tier members earn 3x points, free lounge access, and complimentary seat selection.
24. Points expire after 24 months of account inactivity.
25. Points can be redeemed for flight upgrades at a rate of 10,000 points = ₹5,000 value.

## International Travel Requirements
26. Passengers must carry a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond the return date.
27. Visa requirements vary by destination; travelers are responsible for obtaining visas before departure.
28. Travel insurance is mandatory for all international bookings and costs ₹800 per person per trip.
29. Yellow fever vaccination certificate is required for travel to Sub-Saharan Africa and parts of South America.
30. Schengen area travelers must provide proof of accommodation and return flight at border control.

## Weather and Seasonal Policies
31. Monsoon season (June – September) flights to coastal destinations may experience delays of 1-3 hours.
32. Winter season (December – February) flights to northern destinations may require de-icing, adding 15-30 minutes to departure times.
33. Summer season (April – June) is peak travel time; expect 20-40% higher fares on popular routes.
34. Cyclone alerts may result in automatic rebooking at no additional cost.
35. Snowfall advisories in mountainous regions may require switching to alternate airports.

## Special Assistance
36. Wheelchair assistance must be requested at least 48 hours before departure.
37. Unaccompanied minor service is available for children aged 5-12 at ₹2,500 per segment.
38. Medical oxygen must be arranged through the airline at least 72 hours before departure.
39. Service animals travel free of charge in the cabin with proper documentation.
40. Pregnant travelers beyond 36 weeks require a medical clearance certificate to fly.
