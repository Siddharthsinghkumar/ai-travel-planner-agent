# Regional Travel Guides — Asia Pacific Knowledge Base

1. Japan Rail Pass: The JR Pass allows unlimited travel on most JR trains including Shinkansen (bullet trains) for 7, 14, or 21 consecutive days. Prices start at JPY 29,650 (approximately USD 200) for a 7-day ordinary pass. The pass must be purchased before arriving in Japan and exchanged for the actual pass at a JR office. Note: from October 2023, prices increased by approximately 70%.

2. Japan's Suica and Pasmo cards are rechargeable IC cards that work on most trains, buses, and even at convenience stores and vending machines. They can be purchased at major train stations and are convenient for travel within Tokyo and other major cities. Mobile Suica/Pasmo can be added to Apple Wallet or Google Pay.

3. Southeast Asia budget travel: Daily budgets of USD 25-50 are achievable in Thailand, Vietnam, Cambodia, and Laos for backpackers staying in hostels, eating street food, and using local transport. Mid-range travel (private rooms, restaurant meals, tours) costs USD 50-100 per day. Luxury travel (resorts, fine dining, private tours) costs USD 150-300+ per day.

4. Australia's Electronic Travel Authority (ETA) for US citizens costs AUD 20 and is valid for 12 months with stays of up to 3 months per visit. Processing is typically instant. Australia has strict biosecurity laws — declare all food, plant material, and animal products on arrival. Fines for non-declaration start at AUD 2,664.

5. New Zealand's biosecurity is among the strictest in the world. All visitors must declare outdoor equipment (hiking boots, camping gear) and clean them before arrival. Failure to declare can result in fines up to NZD 100,000 or imprisonment. The NZeTA costs NZD 17 (app) or NZD 23 (web) and includes the IVL (International Visitor Conservation and Tourism Levy) of NZD 35.

6. Singapore's Changi Airport consistently ranks as the world's best airport. It features a butterfly garden, rooftop pool, movie theaters, and the Jewel complex with the world's tallest indoor waterfall. Transit passengers with 5.5+ hours can join a free Singapore tour organized by Changi Airport.

7. India's e-Tourist Visa for US citizens costs USD 25 (30-day), USD 40 (1-year), or USD 80 (5-year) depending on duration. Processing takes 3-5 business days. India has diverse climates — pack accordingly for the region and season. Northern India (Delhi, Rajasthan) can reach 45°C in summer and drop to 5°C in winter.

8. China's visa requirements for US citizens include a 10-year multiple-entry tourist visa costing USD 140. China has a 24/72/144-hour visa-free transit policy at designated airports for US citizens. Major cities (Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen) offer the 144-hour transit. Download essential apps before arrival: WeChat (messaging/payments), Alipay (payments), Didi (ride-hailing), and Baidu Maps.

9. South Korea's K-ETA (Korea Electronic Travel Authorization) costs KRW 10,000 and is valid for 2 years. US citizens are currently exempt from K-ETA requirements through 2025. Seoul's public transportation system (subway and buses) is efficient and affordable — a single ride costs KRW 1,400 (approximately USD 1) with a T-Money card.

10. Thailand's visa exemption for US citizens allows 30 days of stay when arriving by air and 15 days when arriving by land. Extensions of 30 days are available at immigration offices for THB 1,900. Thailand has a "land run" culture where travelers cross a border and re-enter for a new visa exemption, but immigration officers may limit the number of land entries.

11. Bali (Indonesia) requires a visa-on-arrival for US citizens costing IDR 500,000 (approximately USD 32), valid for 30 days with one extension possible. Bali has a tourist tax of IDR 150,000 (approximately USD 10) introduced in 2024. Scooter rental is popular but requires an international driving permit — police frequently fine tourists without proper licenses.

12. Philippines visa-free entry for US citizens allows 30 days of stay. Extensions are available at Bureau of Immigration offices for PHP 3,030 for the first extension (29 days). The Philippines has over 7,000 islands — domestic flights (Cebu Pacific, Philippine Airlines) and ferries (2GO Travel) are the primary means of inter-island travel.

13. Vietnam's e-visa for US citizens costs USD 25 (single entry, 90 days) or USD 50 (multiple entry, 90 days). Processing takes 3-5 business days. Vietnam's train system (Reunification Express) runs from Hanoi to Ho Chi Minh City (30+ hours) and offers scenic coastal views. Domestic flights are affordable (USD 30-80) and much faster for long distances.

14. Malaysia visa-free entry for US citizens allows 90 days of stay. Kuala Lumpur's public transportation (LRT, MRT, monorail) is efficient and affordable. The KLIA Ekspres train connects the airport to the city center in 28 minutes for MYR 55 (approximately USD 12). Malaysia is known for its food diversity — hawker centers offer meals for MYR 5-15.

15. Australia's domestic flight market is dominated by Qantas, Virgin Australia, and Jetstar (budget). Qantas is the full-service carrier with the most extensive network. Jetstar is Qantas's budget subsidiary and charges for checked baggage, seat selection, and meals. Book domestic flights 4-8 weeks in advance for the best prices.
